Where the two diagrams agree

Their RCA lane is our top lane: order, order item, asset, TransactionUsageEntitlement, the asset's rate-card entries and adjustments, and the grants. Their RCB lane is our bottom one: billing schedules created at activation, period items, invoice lines, the invoice, and the write-back on posting. Their "Liable Summary (UBPI) New → ReadyForInvoicing" is exactly the record our bill sync writes.

Where they differ

Their middle — RatingAccount, AccountRatePlan, AccountGrant, Wallets, RatingPeriodItem — is the platform's own rating runtime (RCR), which rates usage inside Revenue Cloud. Ours is m3ter: the order sync creates the account, plan, pricings and attachment there, m3ter meters and rates, and the bill sync carries the rated result back as liable summaries. Our loop never needs TransactionJournal or the Orchestrate Usage Management flow, which is why that row is grey.

What is still open

Which rate card a committed customer's line uses (both reach the asset today). Parent or child bucket on the liable summary. Whether a liable summary invoices without a usage summary behind it. Rates per country as region bands instead of USA / ANY. And the commitment itself — custom quote-line fields in their design, a Balance in m3ter — which is the dashed box in the m3ter lane.
